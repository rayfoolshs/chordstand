Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Chordstand is an offline-first chart library. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no Chordstand server and no account with Chordstand.

What stays on your device

Your songs, setlists, settings, and the app's own storage are kept in your browser's storage (IndexedDB and localStorage) on this device. They are not sent to Chordstand.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose Sign in with Google Drive, Google asks you to authorize the app with the drive.file scope. That scope lets Chordstand create and read only the files it creates in your Drive. It cannot see the rest of your Drive. Backups are written to a Chordstand folder in your Drive.

The app receives an access token and stores it in your browser's session storage. It is removed when you close the tab or press Disconnect. Google's own privacy policy applies to the sign-in and to Drive storage.

No analytics or tracking

The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. It does not collect personal information. The only network requests are the ones you trigger: loading the app, the optional Google sign-in, and the optional Drive backup.

Your control

You can remove your data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your browser, or by deleting the Chordstand folder in your Google Drive. You can revoke the app's access from your Google account's security settings.
